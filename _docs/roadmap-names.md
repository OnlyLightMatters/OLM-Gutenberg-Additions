| -- | --------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1  | Ferruccio Busoni      | One of the earliest visions of a future music freed from traditional constraints and open to new sound possibilities. |
| 2  | Edgard Varèse         | A conception of sound as mass, space, and matter (“organized sound”).                                                 |
| 3  | Luigi Russolo         | A pioneer of the idea that non-musical sounds could become musical material.                                          |
| 4  | Pierre Schaeffer      | Founder of musique concrète and of the idea of listening to sound itself as an object.                                |
| 5  | Karlheinz Stockhausen | Electronic synthesis, spatialization, and exploration of timbre and musical time.                                     |
| 6  | Pierre Henry          | The immersive and physical dimension of electronic sound.                                                             |
| 7  | La Monte Young        | Drone, sustained tones, extended durations, and deep listening: a fundamental root.                                   |
| 8  | Terry Riley           | Loops, cycles, repetition, and altered perceptions of time.                                                           |
| 9  | Morton Feldman        | Extreme slowness, silence, micro-variations, and contemplative listening.                                             |
| 10 | Éliane Radigue        | The heart of the movement: electronic drones, imperceptible transformations, and the spirituality of sound.           |
| 11 | Pauline Oliveros      | Listening as a practice: awareness, attention, and expanded perception of sound.                                      |
| 12 | Alvin Lucier          | Acoustic phenomena, resonance, and the exploration of sound through scientific processes.                             |
| 13 | Steve Reich           | Gradual processes, repetition, and transformation over time.                                                          |
| 14 | Gavin Bryars          | Slowness, melancholy, repetition, and subtle sonic textures.                                                          |
| 15 | Harold Budd           | Fragile minimalism, suspended piano textures, and contemplative atmospheres.                                          |
| 16 | Ryuichi Sakamoto      | A bridge between minimalism, gentle electronics, and contemplative sound worlds.                                      |
| 17 | Hiroshi Yoshimura     | Minimal ambient music, calm sonic spaces, and architectural approaches to sound.                                      |
| 18 | Maryanne Amacher      | Psychoacoustics, physical perception of sound, and immersive listening experiences.                                   |
| 19 | Paul Jebanasam        | A contemporary descendant: deep electronic drones, density, and spatial depth.                                        |
| 20 | Kali Malone           | Contemporary continuation of sustained tones, just intonation, and slow evolving harmonic fields.                     |
| 21 | Iannis Xenakis        | Builder of immersive structures from stochastic processes and mathematical precision.                                 |
